# Sein & Haben - Präteritum 

# 1. The Big Idea: Simple Past for sein & haben
In spoken German, most verbs use the **Perfekt** (hat gemacht, ist gegangen) for the past. However, **sein** (to be) and **haben** (to have) are almost always used in the **Präteritum** (simple past) because it is much shorter and more natural:
- Rather than: **Ich bin gestern zu Hause gewesen.** -> Use: **Ich war gestern zu Hause.** (I was at home yesterday.)
- Rather than: **Ich habe gestern keine Zeit gehabt** -> Use: **Ich hatte gestern keine Zeit.** (I had no time yesterday.)

## Core Meaning Distinction
- **sein** (war): Describes **identity, state/feeling, or location** (Who/where was I? What was I like?)
	- **Example:** Ich **war** müde / in Berlin / Student. (I was tired / in Berlin / a student.)
- **haben** (hatte): Describes **possession, condition or fixed noun expressions** (What did I have?)
	- **Example:** Ich **hatte** ein Auto / Hunger / keine Zeit. (I had a car / hunger \[was hungry] / no time.)
