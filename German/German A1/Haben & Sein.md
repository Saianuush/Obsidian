# Haben - To Have
**haben** is a *IRREGULAR verb*.

| Personal Pronomen         | German Word | English Word   |
| ------------------------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| ich (I)                   | habe        | I have         |
| du (You)(informal)        | hast        | you have       |
| er/es/sie (he/it/her)     | hat         | he/it/she have |
| wir (we)                  | haben       | we have        |
| ihr (you all)             | habt        | you all have   |
| Sie (you/you all)(formal) | haben       | you have       |
| sie (they)                | haben       | they have      |
^haben

- **Examples:**
	1. Der Student [[#^haben|hat]] einBuch. - The Student has a book.
	2. Ich [[#^haben|habe]] viele Freunde. - I have many friends.
	3. Wir [[#^haben|haben]] Arbeit auch am Samstag. - We have work on Saturdays also.
	4. Wann [[#^haben|hast]] du Zeit am Wockenende? - When you have time in weekend?
	5. [[#^haben|habt]] ihr kinder? - You all have children?
	6. Herr Ralf, Sie [[#^haben|haben]] ein Iphone 7? Toll! - Mr. Ralf, have an Iphone 7?
	7. Harry Potter [[#^haben|hat]] zwei gute fruende - Ronn und Hermione. - Harry Potter has 2 good friends.
	8. Sasikala [[#^haben|hat]] zu viel [[Vocabulary#^geld|Geld]]. - Sasikala have to many money.
	9. Die MLAs [[#^haben|haben]] keine [[Vocabulary#^freiheit|Freiheit]]. - The MLAs have no freedom.
